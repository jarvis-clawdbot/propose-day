A Letter from My Heart

February 8, 2026

Hey Fukri Baand,

I know chize bahut badal gye h. I never wished for this na he mai chahata tha aisa kabhe bhe ho. Pr I learned it the hard way ke life mai harr chiz hamare haathme nhi hote h, especially dil sai related chize.

TBH chize bahut galat hue h, pr utna dard nhi h ab. Ye maan liya h maine dil ke chizo mai dimag nhi chalta h, na he chalana chaheye. Pr jab reality hit ho tab dil ko sambhaalna chaheye, as dil bas chize krta reheta h, sunta kese ke nhi h.

Hamare bich huee chize kharab, fir uske baad jo bhe hua, maybe tujhe usme sahe laga ya comfort mila. TBH I do not know what GOD want from us, pr I do know life ke chizo ko lekr decision to leena hame he chaheye.

Mujhe ho raha h bahut bother aise tujhe pareshan dekhkr, and aur zada bura islye lag raha h because mai kuch kar nhi skta hu. Mai apne taraf sai chize krne ke koshish kr sakta hu, pr agar vo tumhe acceptable nhi hoge ya tumhe khushi nhi deege to vo sab waste h.

Jitna I used to know you, tumhe chize bahut bother krte h, tumhe lagta nhi h pr tumhe chaheye hota h tumhe koi samjhaye, sunee tumhare, tumhe akela na chodee. Maybe abhe tumhe ye na lagta ho, jitna mai jaanta hu uske hisab sai bol raha hu.

You are very sensitive and emotional, so tumhe baate bure lag jaate hai and krte h pareshan. And you are very simple person as well, kise sai aise nafrat nhi krte tum, na kise ka bura chahate ho. Normal ladki ho jo khushi chahate h life sai bas.

I know chize abhe samjh nhi aa rahe hoge, pr I want ke tum himmat mat haaro. Give up kabhe nhi krna h life sai, gyan lag raha hoga pr sach hai. Mai aise nhi dekh paa raha hu tumhe.

Tum time le rahe ho usme koi problem nhi h, pr tum time leene ke baad bhe kuch soch nhi rhe ho ya confuse ho uss sai problem hai mujhe. Bahut chize dekh le hai and bahut chize kar le h hamne ab.

Let me ask you this straightaway. Do you want ke ham dono de yek dusre ko firse chance? Fresh start ke lye, past nhi kr skte ham dono control, future ke ending zarur kr sakte h.

I do not know about you, pr tere fikar still h mujhe, tera hona na hona matter karta h mujhe. Tera roona dukh deta h mujhe, tere khushi khush krte h mujhe.

Mujhe nhi pata tere life me mai kya hu ab, kya hai tere mann mai ab. Mujhe nhi pata kya h Bhagwan ka signal tere lye, ya kya tu expect kar rhe h life sai ab. We started everything on 5th march, ye 5th date mere life mai hamesha yek important date rahege.

If you think tu try karne ka soch sakte h, to karte h try. Mai ye nhi bol raha aaj he sab sahe hoga, pr karege dono koshish to zarur ho sakta h. 5th march 2026 tk he krke dekh leete h chize, uss din fir tu mujhe aage ka decision bata deyo.

Jo tujhe sahe lage, jisme tujhe tere khushi lage vo kar. Kuch shuru krne ko nhi bol raha hu, kuch karna h to koshish to krne padege. Dono 5th march tk krke dekh leete h chize.

Bahut kuch dekh liya h hamne, life ne bahut kuch kr liya h hamare sath, ab apne life ke lye he kuch karke dekh leete h. Jo tumhara decision ho mujhe bata dena. Pr mujse discuss zarur karo yek baare ye. I will wait for your message.

Forever yours

"Life ke chizo ko lekr decision to leena hame he chaheye."
